    Methodology & FAQ

    Transparent assumptions, not a black box

    01 / Fully loaded labor

    minutes × days ÷ 60 × 52 × base hourly value × (1 + payroll burden)
    Payroll burden scales labour value; starter tax/payroll presets are directional assumptions.

    02 / Recovered capacity

    loaded labor value × automation × (1 − friction)
    Automation is capped at 90%. This is capacity value—not cash savings.

    03 / Cash ROI vs productivity

    realized cash = recovered capacity × user-set cash share
    Financial ROI and payback stay pre-tax. An optional illustrative tax sensitivity is shown separately and is not used in primary outputs. Redeployed value is shown separately.

    What do the mishap buffer and seasonal variance do?

    The default 15% Operational Mishap & Friction Buffer discounts recovered capacity for exceptions, downtime, onboarding dips, and maintenance rework. Seasonal / Volume Variance (±%) applies the smooth multiplier 1 + variance × sin(2π × monthIndex / 12 − π/2), with monthIndex starting at zero, to status-quo cost and recovered volume. The chart’s shaded band is a user-set sensitivity around modeled financial net—not a statistical confidence interval. This is a planning wave, not a forecast from historical data.

    How do coupled category dynamics rebalance costs?

    When enabled, Pair A Sales ↔ Marketing, Pair B Communication ↔ Meetings, and Pair C Reporting ↔ Data (including Data & Admin) conserve the pair’s prior annual cost as one task’s slider changes. The partner’s target cost becomes the prior pair total minus the edited task’s new cost. Its minutes/day and days/week are selected closest to a cube-root-proportional rebalance; its base hourly value is recalculated to preserve the target cost. Valid bounds still apply, so an extreme input may shift the pair total slightly. All KPIs, shares, charts, scenarios, and report figures recalculate live.

    Why can’t automation recovery reach 100%?

    The recovery assumption is hard-capped at 90%. Human exception handling, QA, privacy checks, and oversight remain in real workflows. The separate mishap buffer discounts that maximum further for operational friction.

    How are jurisdiction, payroll and tax inputs handled?

    Display currency and operating jurisdiction are separate: changing currency never changes tax or payroll assumptions. The jurisdiction selector only labels the operating context; starter inputs change only when you explicitly apply an example rate, and any entered rates remain user assumptions. Payroll burden scales base labour value as an editable planning assumption. Primary financial net, ROI and payback are always pre-tax. If enabled, tax sensitivity applies the entered effective rate directly to modeled cash savings only; it is a what-if, not statutory or taxable-profit modelling, and excludes deductions, credits, losses, entity structure, indirect taxes and jurisdiction-specific rules. Costs receive no tax shield. Confirm treatment with a qualified professional.

    How should I read scenarios, feasibility, and the roadmap?

    Conservative, Base, and Upside are editable stress tests, not benchmarks or percentiles; Base mirrors the live audit, while scenario edits are saved with that audit. The workflow simulator is a separate, temporary overlay and is not saved into the baseline. Feasibility is a transparent heuristic using the editable process, data, integration, judgment, exception, sensitivity, compliance, volume, and criticality inputs—not a guarantee. The 30/60/90 roadmap uses recovered-capacity impact, feasibility, complexity, criticality, duration, dependencies, available lanes, and your manual priorities. Saved Audit stores the baseline locally; Share URL contains the baseline state, not the temporary simulator draft.

    Are exchange rates live, and where is my data stored?

    No. Currency conversions use editable planning rates from USD in the script (USD 1, INR 83.50, EUR 0.92, GBP 0.79). Audit data and saved snapshots stay in browser localStorage; no backend is used. A shared URL contains the encoded audit state in its hash.

    Privacy, encryption & backups

    All operations run in this browser. No password or key is transmitted.

    Security limits. Optional AES-256-GCM encryption protects data stored by this app while the workspace is locked. It does not protect against malicious scripts running in an unlocked browser, a compromised device, browser extensions, or someone controlling your session. This is not end-to-end encryption. If you lose your password, this app cannot recover encrypted data.
