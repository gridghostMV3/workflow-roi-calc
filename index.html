    Methodology & FAQ

    Transparent assumptions, not a black box

    01 / Fully loaded labor

    minutes × days ÷ 60 × 52 × base hourly value × (1 + payroll burden)
    Payroll/social charges are included before estimating recovered capacity.

    02 / Risk-adjusted recovery

    loaded labor cost × recovery % × (1 − mishap buffer)
    Recovery is capped at 90%; the seasonal wave changes monthly volume.

    03 / Net impact

    recovered benefit − setup − 12 × monthly tools
    After-tax impact taxes the benefit and assumes no tax shield on costs.

    What do the mishap buffer and seasonal variance do?

    The default 15% Operational Mishap & Friction Buffer discounts recovered capacity for exceptions, downtime, onboarding dips, and maintenance rework. Seasonal / Volume Variance (±%) applies the smooth multiplier 1 + variance × sin(2π × monthIndex / 12 − π/2), with monthIndex starting at zero, to status-quo cost and recovered volume. The chart shades a confidence band by applying the same selected variance above and below each month’s expected recovered value. It is a planning wave, not a forecast from historical data.

    How do coupled category dynamics rebalance costs?

    When enabled, Pair A Sales ↔ Marketing, Pair B Communication ↔ Meetings, and Pair C Reporting ↔ Data (including Data & Admin) conserve the pair’s prior annual cost as one task’s slider changes. The partner’s target cost becomes the prior pair total minus the edited task’s new cost. Its minutes/day and days/week are selected closest to a cube-root-proportional rebalance; its base hourly value is recalculated to preserve the target cost. Valid bounds still apply, so an extreme input may shift the pair total slightly. All KPIs, shares, charts, scenarios, and report figures recalculate live.

    Why can’t automation recovery reach 100%?

    The recovery assumption is hard-capped at 90%. Human exception handling, QA, privacy checks, and oversight remain in real workflows. The separate mishap buffer discounts that maximum further for operational friction.

    Which tax and payroll presets are applied?

    Changing currency selects a regional planning preset unless you have made a custom override: US Federal + State ≈25% effective corporate tax / 12% employer payroll burden; India 25.17% / 12% PF-overhead; EU blended 25% / 20% social charges; UK 25% / 13.8% employer NI. Payroll burden multiplies each task’s base hourly value to estimate fully-loaded labor cost. Corporate tax applies to recovered benefit only; setup and monthly tool costs are subtracted after tax without assuming a deductible-cost tax shield. These are editable estimates, not tax advice.

    How should I read scenarios, feasibility, and the roadmap?

    Conservative, Base, and Upside cases are what-if views derived from your live sliders; they do not overwrite your audit. Feasibility is a transparent 0–100 heuristic based on recurrence, annual effort, digital category fit, and repeatability—not a guarantee. The 30/60/90 roadmap orders pilots by value and readiness. Save Audit stores a local snapshot in this browser; Share URL serializes the current audit into the link.

    Are exchange rates live, and where is my data stored?

    No. Currency conversions use editable planning rates from USD in the script (USD 1, INR 83.50, EUR 0.92, GBP 0.79). Audit data and saved snapshots stay in browser localStorage; no backend is used. A shared URL contains the encoded audit state in its hash.
